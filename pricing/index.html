04 / Before booking

Everything agreed upfront

These are guide prices for straightforward residential work. We confirm the exact scope and total cost before booking.

01

What's included

Professional image selection, standard editing, suitable interior/exterior coverage, online digital delivery and one reasonable correction round for still photographs.

02

Property & travel

Size, layout, difficult access, location and parking can affect the quotation. Larger holiday lets, complex HMOs and commercial premises may need a bespoke price.

03

Timing & deposit

Still photographs usually arrive within 24–48 hours. A 25% deposit normally confirms a booking; the balance is due before final delivery unless agreed otherwise.

04

Drone & specialist work

Drone work requires suitable conditions, safe access and relevant permissions. Tour hosting, video output formats and unusual deliverables are agreed in your quote.

What if I need 15, 25 or more than 32 photographs?

There are no gaps between packages: Standard includes up to 22 photos, and Premium up to 32. Extra edited photographs can be requested at £6 each when captured during the same shoot. If the brief is more extensive, we quote the necessary time before booking.

Can I combine photography, drone, video and floor plans?

Yes. The published bundles cover photography with either drone or a floor plan; for three or more services, we provide a coordinated quotation.

Do you offer ongoing rates for estate agents?

For repeat or multi-property bookings, we can discuss a tailored rate based on actual volume, location and property scope. Occasional bookings use the published guide prices.

What if I need to rearrange my booking?

With at least 72 hours' notice, deposits can usually be transferred once to a new date. Cancellation charges and any supplier commitments are confirmed with your booking terms.

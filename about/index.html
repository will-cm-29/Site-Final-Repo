About MW Photography

MW Photography is a South Coast property-media business run by Will and Elliot, serving property, holiday-let and hospitality clients across Hampshire and Dorset.

Request a quoteView projects

Our approach

We plan each shoot around the property and where the media will be used. That means balancing attractive composition with practical coverage of layout, access, amenities and location.

Editing is kept consistent across the full set, with attention to colour, brightness, vertical lines and natural presentation.

What we provide

  • Interior and exterior property photography
  • Drone photography and video subject to safe operating conditions
  • Property walkthrough video and social formats
  • Hospitality and commercial interior imagery

About MW Photography

Property media with a clear commercial purpose.

MW Photography is run by Will and Elliot. We create photography, drone imagery and video for property, holiday-let and hospitality clients across the South Coast.

Work with usView our work

Our approach

Attractive imagery is not enough on its own.

The media still needs to explain the property, answer practical questions and work on the platform where it will appear. We balance composition with complete coverage of layout, amenities, access and location.

That principle carries through the edit. Colour, brightness and vertical lines are handled consistently, without pushing the property into an artificial or misleading presentation.

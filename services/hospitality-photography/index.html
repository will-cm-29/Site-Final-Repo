Common questions

Questions about this service

Practical answers to the points that most often affect scope, preparation and delivery.

What types of hospitality businesses do you photograph?

The service is suitable for hotels, pubs, restaurants, bars, venues and other guest-facing businesses that need clear interior, exterior, atmosphere and detail imagery.

Can a shoot be planned around opening hours?

Yes. Quiet access before opening or during a low-traffic period usually gives better control over room presentation, lighting and unwanted people in the frame.

Can photography and video be captured during one visit?

They can be coordinated where the brief and available time are realistic. The scope should identify priority spaces, required formats and whether operational activity needs to be shown.

Do you provide images for both websites and social media?

Yes. Delivery can include high-resolution files and web-ready versions, with the intended platforms agreed before the shoot.

Common questions

Questions about this service

Practical answers to the points that most often affect scope, preparation and delivery.

How many photographs will I receive?

The final image count depends on the property size, layout and agreed scope. The quote will state the expected coverage rather than relying on a fixed number that may not suit the property.

How should the property be prepared?

Rooms should be clean, uncluttered and ready before arrival. Personal items, bins, cables and temporary signage are best removed so the shoot can focus on the property rather than staging work.

Can photography and drone imagery be completed together?

Yes. Where the location, weather, airspace and permissions allow, ground photography and aerial imagery can be coordinated within the same visit.

What file formats are supplied?

Edited images are delivered digitally in web-ready and high-resolution formats appropriate to the agreed marketing use.

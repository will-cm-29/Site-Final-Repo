Common questions

Questions about this service

Practical answers to the points that most often affect scope, preparation and delivery.

What should an Airbnb photography gallery include?

A useful gallery should show the main rooms, sleeping arrangements, bathrooms, kitchen, exterior, access, amenities and any features that materially affect a guest’s decision.

Can you photograph a property between guest stays?

Yes, provided the property is fully cleaned, staged and accessible for the agreed window. A realistic buffer is important because rushed turnovers often reduce the quality of the finished gallery.

Do you photograph small details as well as rooms?

Yes. Detail images can support the wider gallery when they communicate a genuine feature, amenity or sense of the stay rather than replacing essential room coverage.

Can drone photography be added to a holiday-let shoot?

Yes, where aerial imagery adds useful context and the flight can be completed safely and lawfully. It is most valuable for grounds, coastline, setting and access.

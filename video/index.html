Before You Book

Delivery

Standard delivery is usually 2–4 working days. Priority turnaround may be available from £15 depending on the package, edit complexity and workload.

Deposit

A 25% deposit is required to secure the booking. The remaining balance is due before final delivery.

AI disclosure

AI-assisted photo video is clearly offered as a separate service from filmed videography. It uses still images, AI animation tools and manual editing.

Longer edits

Videos longer than 2 minutes, additional cut-downs and platform-specific versions are available by request and quoted based on the brief.

Property prep

For filmed video, please ensure the property is ready at the agreed arrival time. Significant delays on site may shorten the available shoot window.

Travel

Based on the South Coast, working across Hampshire, Dorset and London. Travel costs may apply depending on distance and access requirements.

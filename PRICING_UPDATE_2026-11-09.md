# MW Photography website pricing update — 9 November 2026

## Package rates implemented
- Photography Entry: £89, up to 12 final edited ground photos.
- Photography Standard: £119, up to 22 final edited ground photos.
- Photography Premium: £159, up to 32 final edited ground photos.
- Photography + floor plan: £119 / £149 / £199 (same ground photo allowance + one marketing plan).
- Photography + drone: £159 / £209 / £259 (same ground-photo allowance + respectively up to 5 / 8 / 12 additional edited aerial photographs).
- Extra edited photos captured at the same shoot: £6 each, where available; otherwise a bespoke quote.
- Drone-only, video, 360 tour and floor-plan standalone opening rates unchanged.

## Design and technical changes
- New mobile-first editorial pricing page. Prices and inclusions appear inline, no sideways-scrolling comparison tables or pop-ups.
- Navigation anchors link to packages, bundles, other media and booking information.
- Homepage pricing overlays and FAQ updated to match rates and included photo counts.
- Contact enquiry preselects service and preserves tier/price context when clicked from the pricing page.
- Reused authentic photography assets already in the website; no generated images.
- Removed unused old pricing-page.js modal controller.

## Owner checks before publishing
- Confirm VAT registration/advertised VAT treatment and any applicable VAT disclosures. Current website does not assert a registration status.
- Confirm the £6 rate for images captured at the same visit, and whether tier photo counts should be treated as contractual caps for all shoots.
- Confirm one complimentary round of *reasonable corrections* (not a new editing style or re-shoot) for stills packages.
- Confirm capacity to supply floor plans and drone counts at advertised bundles, especially for Premium.
- Ensure travel/parking, cancellation/refund terms and 25% deposits match your booking agreements.
- Check Formspree email delivery using a test enquiry.

Holiday-let photography should stay aligned with the guest experience. If a property changes, or if seasonal appeal is a major part of the booking decision, refreshed photography can help the listing feel current and accurate.

Refresh after major updates

If you redecorate, replace furniture, improve lighting or change the layout, your images should reflect the new guest experience. Old photos can create confusion and may undersell the property after investment has been made.

Update outdoor images when they matter

Gardens, terraces, balconies and coastal settings can be major selling points. If outdoor spaces look best at a particular time of year, seasonal photography can support summer campaigns, family holidays or weekend breaks.

Photograph new amenities

Hot tubs, workspaces, upgraded kitchens, EV charging, outdoor dining areas and pet-friendly features should be photographed clearly. Guests often filter or compare listings based on practical amenities, so these details deserve strong images.

Consider different campaigns

A gallery designed for peak summer may not be ideal for winter stays, corporate bookings or off-season weekend breaks. Having a broader set of images gives you more flexibility across platforms and promotions.

Keep accuracy at the centre

A refreshed gallery should still represent the property honestly. The purpose is to show the current stay clearly, not to create a version of the property that guests will not recognise on arrival.

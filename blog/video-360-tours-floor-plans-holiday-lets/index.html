Still photography is usually the foundation of a holiday-let listing, but video, 360 tours and floor plans can add extra clarity. They are especially useful when layout, scale or experience is difficult to explain through photographs alone.

Use video to show atmosphere and movement

Video can help communicate how a property feels, especially when there are views, outdoor areas, large open-plan spaces or lifestyle details. It is useful for social media, websites and campaigns where movement can create stronger attention.

Use 360 tours for confidence

A 360 tour allows potential guests to explore the property more actively. It can be useful for larger homes, serviced accommodation, premium stays or properties where layout is a frequent guest question.

Use floor plans to explain layout

Floor plans help guests understand room relationships, bedroom locations, bathrooms and access. They are particularly useful for family stays, group bookings and properties with multiple floors.

Do not replace still photography

Video, 360 tours and floor plans should support a strong image set. Most guests still make their first judgement from photographs, so the core gallery needs to be clear and polished.

Choose services based on the property

A compact apartment may only need strong stills. A large coastal home may benefit from photography, drone images, video and a floor plan. The right package depends on what guests need to understand before booking.

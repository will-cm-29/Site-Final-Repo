A strong Airbnb gallery should answer guest questions quickly. It should show what the property looks like, how it works and why it is worth choosing over similar listings. This checklist gives hosts a practical structure for planning a complete image set.

Choose a clear hero image

Your first image should show the most compelling reason to click. That might be a beautiful living space, a bedroom with a view, a stylish kitchen, an outdoor area or a memorable exterior. It should be bright, straight, uncluttered and easy to understand at thumbnail size.

Cover every main room

Guests expect to see the living area, bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchen, dining space and any additional areas such as workspaces or games rooms. Missing rooms can create doubt. A complete gallery reassures guests that the listing is transparent.

Show amenities that influence booking decisions

Photograph useful features such as parking, outdoor seating, hot tubs, desks, coffee machines, family equipment, laundry facilities, views and pet-friendly spaces. Amenities are often part of the reason a guest chooses one property over another.

Include exterior and arrival images

The front of the property, entrance and outdoor spaces help guests understand arrival and setting. These images are especially useful for houses, rural stays, coastal accommodation and properties where location is part of the appeal.

Order the gallery like a walkthrough

Start with the strongest images, then guide the viewer through the main spaces. Avoid grouping too many similar angles together. A good order feels natural and gives a balanced sense of the whole stay.

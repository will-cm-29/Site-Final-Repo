The best property photography starts before the camera comes out. A well-prepared holiday let is easier to photograph, looks more considered and helps the final gallery feel cleaner, brighter and more inviting.

Declutter before styling

Remove everyday clutter before adding decorative touches. Remote controls, cables, bins, toiletries, paperwork and excess kitchen items can distract from the space. The aim is not to make the property feel empty, but to make every visible item feel intentional.

Prepare bedrooms carefully

Beds are often some of the most important images in a holiday-let gallery. Use clean, pressed bedding where possible, dress pillows evenly and check that throws or cushions look balanced. A bedroom should look restful, spacious and ready for a guest to arrive.

Make kitchens and bathrooms spotless

Kitchens and bathrooms show how well a property is maintained. Clear draining boards, remove cleaning products, polish taps and mirrors, align towels and hide personal items. Small marks that seem minor in person can become very visible in high-resolution photography.

Think about light and access

Open curtains and blinds, check bulbs are working and make sure the photographer can move freely around each room. If a space has a strong view, clear the window area and remove anything that blocks the sightline.

Do not forget the outside

Entrances, gardens, patios, driveways and balconies all form part of the guest experience. Sweep paths, tidy outdoor furniture, remove bins from sight and make sure cushions or seating areas look ready to use.

Bournemouth Airbnb photography

Bournemouth Airbnb with private pool

A complete interior, exterior and amenity-focused gallery for a spacious Bournemouth holiday let. The final selection was curated to show the modern kitchen, dining and conservatory spaces, multiple bedrooms and bathrooms, landscaped garden and private outdoor swimming pool without repeating near-identical angles.

Location

Bournemouth, Dorset

Project type

Airbnb and holiday-let photography

Useful for

Listings, direct-booking websites and social media

Selected gallery

Sixteen photographs selected from the completed shoot, with duplicate bathroom, conservatory, pool and exterior angles removed to keep the presentation concise.

Project approach

Showing the experience as well as the rooms

The gallery was structured around the questions a potential guest is likely to have: what the principal social spaces feel like, how many distinct sleeping areas are available, the standard of the bathrooms, and what makes the property different from competing Bournemouth stays.

Lead images

The blue kitchen, glazed conservatory and private pool provide the strongest differentiators and are suitable for prominent use in the listing gallery.

Complete coverage

Bedrooms, bathrooms, storage, circulation spaces, exterior access and small welcome details give guests a more complete understanding of the stay.

Straightforward pricing

Guide pricing for property photography, video and marketing services based on the South Coast, working across Southampton, Hampshire, Dorset and London. Final quotes depend on property size, location, access, required deliverables and turnaround, but these prices provide a clear starting point.

Notes

  • Listed prices apply to standard-scope bookings.
  • Larger properties, complex shoots, additional deliverables, difficult access, urgent turnaround, travel, parking or congestion charges may affect the final quote.
  • Same-day priority starts from £15 and is quoted depending on shoot size, service type, edit complexity and workload.
  • Travel may affect the final quote depending on distance, parking and access.
  • Parking costs, congestion charges, access delays or restricted entry may affect the final quote.
  • Drone work is subject to weather, airspace restrictions and permissions.
  • Combined photography packages are priced as booking deals and are intended to be better value than booking the same services separately.
  • Drone footage is included with walkthrough video packages, subject to weather, permissions, airspace restrictions and safe operating conditions.
  • Walkthrough video packages can include optional realtor / agent overlays such as room details, bedroom counts and key property features. Longer videos and shorter platform-specific cut-downs are available by request.
  • Weekend and urgent bookings are available by request and quoted depending on availability.
  • Larger residential, hospitality, commercial and multi-site projects are quoted individually.
  • Regular client and multi-property rates are available for estate agents, landlords, developers and portfolio owners.

Booking Information

  • A 25% deposit is required to secure the booking.
  • The remaining balance is due before final delivery.
  • Deposits are non-refundable for cancellations made less than 72 hours before the booking.
  • With at least 72 hours’ notice, a deposit can usually be transferred once to a new date.

MW Photography

Professional photography for property, hospitality and commercial spaces.

We provide property, hospitality and commercial photography based on the South Coast, working across Southampton, Hampshire, Dorset and London, with a focus on clean composition, natural light and accurate presentation.

Our work is created for listings, websites and marketing materials, with an emphasis on strong composition, clarity and imagery that feels polished without looking over-processed.

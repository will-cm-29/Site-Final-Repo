Before You Book

Existing photos

If you supply suitable images, this can usually be handled remotely without a site visit or shoot cancellation policy.

New photography

If you need MW Photography to visit and shoot new images, the standard photography package price, deposit and cancellation terms apply.

Editing start

For supplied-photo projects, there is no shoot to cancel, but the editing fee may not be refundable once editing has started.

Image quality

Low-resolution, heavily compressed or poorly lit photos may limit the final result. The best results come from strong, clean property images.

AI disclosure

AI-assisted tools add movement to the images. MW Photography completes the edit, pacing, music, title treatment and final delivery.

Not filmed video

This is separate from walkthrough video. For a true filmed walkthrough, drone footage and on-site movement, use the walkthrough video service.

Send over the property photos or property details and we can confirm whether Still Image Motion or a filmed walkthrough is the better fit.
